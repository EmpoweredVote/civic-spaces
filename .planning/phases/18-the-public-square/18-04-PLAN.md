# Phase 18-04: The closed door and the explainer

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Someone who has just read a conversation they cannot reply to is told why, quietly, in the place the reply box would be — and can find out what the account tiers mean if they want to.

**Architecture:** One new component, `ReplyClosedNotice`, renders in the slot `ReplyComposer` and the FAB occupy, driven by the reason `whyNoWrite` already returns. One new route, `/accounts`, holds the explainer. Nothing is disabled, nothing is red, and the explainer is reached by pressing the door rather than pushed in front of it.

**Tech Stack:** React 19, wouter, Tailwind v4, `motion/react`, `@empoweredvote/ev-ui`; Playwright (global install) for the four-way visual pass.

**Spec:** `.planning/phases/18-the-public-square/18-CONTEXT.md` — D-30, D-35, D-36, D-37, D-38, D-39. Tier copy is authoritative in `empowered-vote-primer.md` § Account Architecture.

**Depends on:** 18-03 (there is no point telling someone they cannot reply to a conversation they cannot see).

## Global Constraints

- **The copy, exactly:** the sentence is **"Replying is open to Connected accounts."** and the button reads **"Connect an account to reply"**. Chris fixed both.
- 🔴 **Never disabled, never `cursor-not-allowed`, no dead ends.** `check:explore-rooms` goal 6 forbids both outright across this surface. The control is live; it opens the explainer.
- 🔴 **Not coral, not filled, not urgent.** Chris: *"Not everyone wants to login, and that's ok. We don't need something bright red constantly reminding them to do something they don't want to do."* Teal outline (`#00657C` family), no fill.
- **The button opens the explainer, never a login form.** D-37: the explainer comes after the attempt. A direct jump to the accounts hub is the thing being replaced.
- **Nothing advertises an opt-in** (D-33). No badge, no chip, no "this member shares…" anywhere on this surface.
- **Light-mode text is `gray-900` / `gray-600` / `gray-500`; `gray-400` is decoration only.** In dark mode `gray-500` fails and `gray-400` is the floor. 🔴 Check contrast with a canvas `fillRect` + `getImageData` — Tailwind v4 emits `oklch()`, and reading `ctx.fillStyle` back hands you the oklch string parsed as RGB, which reports ~1:1 for perfectly legible text.
- **Dark mode is the default and is class-based**; write every light value with its `dark:` counterpart in the same edit.

## Review Focus

Input classes the spec implies that no task's assertions would otherwise reach:

1. **A Connected member reading a sibling shard of their own jurisdiction.** `whyNoWrite` returns `'sibling-shard'`, which is neither "sign in" nor "you don't live here" — the notice must say something true for that case rather than falling through to the signed-out copy. Covered in Task 1, Step 1.
2. **A member with no jurisdiction at all** (`'no-slice'` — the unincorporated case). Telling them "replying is open to Connected accounts" when they *have* one is a lie; they need the accounts-app route, not the explainer. Covered in Task 1, Step 1.
3. **An Inform account.** It reaches `'signed-out'` by construction (D-04) and must receive the *same* notice as a visitor, not a different one — a tier-specific message here would be the first place in the codebase where Inform and anon diverge. Covered in Task 1, Step 1.
4. **The notice on a narrow phone.** It sits where a composer was; a two-line sentence plus a button must not push the last reply off-screen or overflow the card. Covered in Task 2, Step 4.
5. **Reaching `/accounts` directly, signed out, with no referrer.** It is a real URL people will share; it must render standalone and offer a way back into the square rather than dead-ending. Covered in Task 3, Step 3.

### Task 1: The reason-to-copy mapping

**Files:**
- Modify: `src/lib/writeAccess.ts` (add `replyClosedCopy`)
- Modify: `scripts/check-write-access.mjs` (add assertions 37–42)

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `NoWriteReason` and `whyNoWrite` from `writeAccess.ts`.
- Produces: `replyClosedCopy(reason: NoWriteReason): { sentence: string; action: 'explainer' | 'accounts-app' | null; actionLabel: string | null }`. Task 2 renders whatever this returns and decides nothing itself.

🔴 It lives in `writeAccess.ts` because that is where the reasons are decided, and because `check-write-access.mjs` runs the shipped module. A mapping kept in the component is a mapping no check can reach — the same argument D-21's seam comment makes.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing assertions**

```js
check("signed-out says replying is open to Connected accounts",
  replyClosedCopy('signed-out').sentence === 'Replying is open to Connected accounts.')
check("signed-out offers the explainer",
  replyClosedCopy('signed-out').action === 'explainer' &&
  replyClosedCopy('signed-out').actionLabel === 'Connect an account to reply')
check("an Inform account gets the signed-out copy verbatim (D-04)",
  JSON.stringify(replyClosedCopy(whyNoWrite(informAtCity))) ===
  JSON.stringify(replyClosedCopy('signed-out')))
check("other-place names the place rule, not the account rule",
  replyClosedCopy('other-place').sentence.includes('live') &&
  !replyClosedCopy('other-place').sentence.includes('Connected accounts'))
check("sibling-shard is its own sentence",
  replyClosedCopy('sibling-shard').sentence !== replyClosedCopy('other-place').sentence)
check("no-slice routes to the accounts app, not the explainer",
  replyClosedCopy('no-slice').action === 'accounts-app')
```

- [ ] **Step 2: Run it and verify it fails**

Expected: 36 PASS (from 18-03), then 6 FAIL — `replyClosedCopy is not a function`.

- [ ] **Step 3: Implement `replyClosedCopy`**

A total function over the four `NoWriteReason` values — exhaustive, with no `default` branch, so adding a fifth reason later is a type error rather than a silent fallthrough. Exact strings:

- `'signed-out'` → `'Replying is open to Connected accounts.'`, explainer, `'Connect an account to reply'`
- `'other-place'` → a sentence saying posting belongs to the people who live under that government; explainer; `'Connect an account to reply'`
- `'sibling-shard'` → a sentence saying this is another room of the same place and the viewer's own room is where they post; action `null`
- `'no-slice'` → a sentence saying no space exists here yet; `'accounts-app'`; a label pointing at `app.empowered.vote/settings/location`

### Task 3: The `/accounts` explainer

**Files:**
- Create: `src/components/AccountsExplainer.tsx`
- Modify: `src/App.tsx` (add `<Route path="/accounts">`)
- Modify: `src/components/ReplyClosedNotice.tsx` (wire `onExplain` to navigate there)

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: the tier model in `empowered-vote-primer.md` § Account Architecture.
- Produces: the `/accounts` route.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the page**

Three tiers — Inform, Connect, Empowered — what each is for and how they differ, and plainly that taking part in Civic Spaces needs a Connected account. Source the substance from `empowered-vote-primer.md`, not from memory. Two things it must say because they are the actual ceiling: Connected requires an address, because slice assignment runs off encrypted coordinates; and Connected is a **pseudonymous** tier — identity is private, which is protection from a stalker, **not** licence to be toxic, because there is a paper trail.

🔴 It must also carry D-39's scoping, which is the part a reader will be surprised by: a Connected account replies **in its own slice**. Reading another place and being unable to answer there is the rule, not a bug.

- [ ] **Step 2: Note the tier-model discrepancy rather than resolving it**

`empowered-vote-primer.md` says *"Presence of a child record IS the tier. No record = Inform."* `civic_spaces.connected_profiles` carries a `tier text CHECK (tier IN ('connected','inform'))` column instead. These disagree. 🔴 Do not reconcile them here and do not make the page depend on either: this phase's rules are membership-based and need no tier signal at all (D-48). Leave a comment in the component naming the discrepancy and pointing at ev-accounts, so the next person does not quietly pick one.

- [ ] **Step 3: Make it standalone**

It renders signed out, with no referrer, as a shareable URL (Review Focus 5), and offers a way back into the square. No login form, no redirect on load.

- [ ] **Step 4: Verify**

Run: `npm run build`, then load `/accounts` directly in a fresh signed-out context and screenshot four ways.
Expected: build clean; the page renders standalone in all four; every tier named; no dead end.
