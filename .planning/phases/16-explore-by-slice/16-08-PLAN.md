# Phase 16-08 — The invitation to seed an unseeded place, shown without the vote

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development
> (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps
> use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A member who opens Explore for a place nobody has written a word about meets a
welcome that names their place, says what this space becomes, and points at things they can
actually do right now — and never at a control that does nothing.

**Scope note, 2026-10-24.** Chris: *"show it without the vote for now."* The seed
**invitation** is in scope. The seed **vote** — recording which issues a member picks — is a
write, remains out of scope, and remains its own later plan: nothing here stores anything,
and nothing here pretends to.

**Architecture:** 16-01's resolver already computes `isUnseeded` — true for a real place with
no bespoke layer, false for a placeless level and false for a viewer with no geoid, who
cannot seed a place the app cannot name. This plan does three things: moves
`seed-invitation` to the top of the composition when `isUnseeded`, builds the module, and
adds a committed check for the one failure mode that would make it worse than nothing.

🔴 **The design resolution, and the reason this is honest rather than a "coming soon" box.**
Decision 11 describes an invitation you *press* to see the prominent issues for your place
and vote on them. Without the vote, pressing would show issues that are already on the page
below. So the press and the vote arrive together, and what ships now is the half that is
fully true today: **the framing**. The invitation names the place, explains that the
questions below are the ones that come up most places rather than anything written about
here, says that choosing what this space focuses on arrives next, and offers **one real
control that really works** — opening the first of those questions. The page beneath it is
already full of topics a member can open and start a conversation in. That is a welcome, not
a dead end, and none of it is a promise we are not keeping.

## The failure mode this module must not have

`16-CONTEXT.md` names it, and it is the acceptance criterion:

> **It must not present a live-looking control that no-ops** — that is the one version that
> is genuinely the dead end, and it is the failure mode to verify against.

Four shapes it could take. All four are prohibited:

| Prohibited | Why |
|---|---|
| A **disabled** button ("Choose what matters here", `opacity-50 cursor-not-allowed`) | The generic UX advice for a non-interactive control is to dim it and change the cursor. That advice is for a control that is *temporarily* unavailable to *you*. This one is unavailable to everyone, forever, until a later phase — and dimmed-out it reads as "broken" or "you are not allowed", which is apologetic and also untrue. |
| An **enabled** button that stores nothing | A member who is told their voice counted when nothing was stored has been lied to. This is the version the design's own reasoning rules out. |
| Radio buttons or chips that select and then evaporate on reload | The same lie with extra steps, and worse: it looks like it worked. |
| A card that says only **"Coming soon"** | The dead end goal 6 forbids, in its purest form. |

What is permitted, and what this plan builds: **real content, one real control, and one
plain sentence about what arrives next.** If a thing on this card can be clicked, clicking it
does something observable. If a thing cannot be done yet, it is described in prose and is not
shaped like a control.

### Task 2: Build the invitation

**Files:**
- Create: `src/components/explore/modules/SeedInvitationModule.tsx`
- Modify: `src/components/explore/registry.tsx`
- Reference (do not edit): `src/components/explore/exploreUi.tsx` — the whole vocabulary;
  `ExploreActivities.tsx:43-55` — the Imagine card's header, the closest existing analogue
  for "a card that explains itself before it asks anything"

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `ExploreCtx` — `placeName`, `composition`, `openTopic`, `isViewOnly`.
- Produces: one registry entry.

- [ ] **Step 1: Layout**

One `CARD`, matching the Imagine and Nearby cards in width and rhythm so it reads as part of
the page rather than a notice pasted on top of it. A two-track grid from `@xl` (an
illustration or a quiet brand-tinted panel on one side, the words on the other), stacking to
one column below — the same `@container` shape `ImagineActivity` uses. Reuse its container
query breakpoints; do not pick new ones.

Hierarchy, top to bottom:

1. **Eyebrow** — `text-brand dark:text-brand-light`, the same uppercase tracking every
   Explore section opener uses. Something like `A new civic space`.
2. **Heading** — the place, named. This is the welcome and it carries the weight. Match the
   `SectionHeader` heading scale; do not introduce a bigger one.
3. **One paragraph, two sentences.** What this space is for, and that nobody has shaped it
   yet. Max 65–75 characters per line (`max-w-2xl` already achieves this at the body size) —
   a wall of text is the "overwhelming the member" anti-goal.
4. **The honest line about the questions below.** See Step 3 — the wording matters more than
   the layout.
5. **One control.** See Step 2.
6. **The "arrives next" line**, in the small-print voice (`text-xs`, `gray-500` /
   `dark:gray-400`). Prose, in the DOM — not a tooltip, not a `title` attribute, not revealed
   on hover. A screen reader and a phone must both get it.

Tone, per decision 9: welcoming, not overwhelming, and a bit fun. The bar is that
contributing looks small enough to do on the way past. Nothing here should read as a form, a
commitment or a job — and right now nothing here asks for anything at all, which makes that
easy. Do not manufacture obligation to fill the space.

- [ ] **Step 2: The one control, and it is real**

A single `PrimaryButton` (or `SecondaryButton` — judge it against the page; the featured
card above already has the loud one) that **opens the first candidate topic**:
`ctx.openTopic(ctx.composition.featuredTopicId ?? ctx.composition.questionTopicIds[0])`.

It works today. It is the action goal 6 actually wants — joining is a reaction rather than a
blank page — and it is the thing the member can do about this empty space right now.

Guard it: if there is no topic to open, render **no button at all** rather than a dead one.
Never `disabled`. Never `aria-disabled`. Never `cursor-not-allowed`. Anywhere in this file.

Accessibility, all of it already available in the repo's vocabulary:
- `<section aria-labelledby>` with the heading's id.
- The `FOCUS` constant from `exploreUi.tsx` — it is `focus-visible:ring-2` with an offset,
  which is the replacement an `outline-none` requires.
- ≥44×44px touch target. `PrimaryButton`'s `lg` and `card` sizes already clear this; `md`
  at `py-2` does not on its own — check it rather than assuming.
- Any decorative SVG gets `aria-hidden="true"`; an icon-only control would need an
  `aria-label`, so do not make one.
- SVG icons only. No emoji as an icon.
- Any entrance animation goes through `motion/react` with a `motion-safe:` guard, 150–300 ms,
  on `transform` and `opacity` only. A welcome that slides in for someone who asked the OS to
  stop things moving is not welcoming.

- [ ] **Step 3: 🔴 The copy trap**

The questions on an unseeded page are the by-level generic set, ordered by `rankByCompass` —
which uses the **viewing member's own** Compass answers and nothing else.

**Never write copy that attributes the ordering to the people who live there.** Not *"what
people here care about"*, not *"your neighbours' priorities"*, not *"the issues this
community cares about most"*. Each of those describes a slice-wide aggregate of members'
political answers. **No such aggregate exists, no endpoint serves one, and locked decision 7
says none should be designed** — precisely so the privacy question that comes with it stays
closed. A sentence is all it would take to promise one.

What is true, and what to write: these questions come up in most places; nobody has written
anything specific to this one yet; if you have answered Compass, the ones nearest your own
answers come first.

And if the member has answered fewer than `MIN_RANKED` (3) topics, `rankByCompass` returns
source order untouched — so that last clause must be conditional on there being a ranking, or
it is a small lie told to most members.

- [ ] **Step 4: The "arrives next" sentence**

One sentence, concrete, no date:

> Soon you will be able to tell us which of these this space should focus on, and ask for one
> that is missing.

Then stop. No countdown, no "coming soon" badge, no waitlist, no notify-me — a notify-me is a
write, and it is the second prohibited shape. Stating what is next and then stopping is the
whole technique: it is a plan, told plainly, which is different from a promise with a shape
that invites a press.

- [ ] **Step 5: Registry entry**

```ts
available: (ctx) => ctx.composition.isUnseeded && !!ctx.placeName,
emptyState: 'hide',
```

`placeName` is in `available` on purpose: an invitation that cannot name the place is not an
invitation. Hiding until the name resolves is a frame or two of nothing, which is better than
a sentence with a gap in it.

```bash
npm run build
git add src/components/explore/modules/SeedInvitationModule.tsx src/components/explore/registry.tsx
git commit -m "feat(explore): welcome an unseeded place by name, with no control that no-ops"
```

**Files:**
- Create: `scripts/check-explore-rooms.mjs`
- Modify: `package.json` — `"check:explore-rooms": "node scripts/check-explore-rooms.mjs"`

- [ ] **Step 1: Write it**

Same shape as 16-05's `check-explore-gate.mjs`: the shared `loadChromium()`, a `--port`
argument, page-error collection, no credential — it drives `?dev=1`.

Header comment:

> Every non-interactive room in Explore — the seed invitation and the reserved rooms — must
> contain no control that does nothing. A disabled button, or an enabled one that stores
> nothing, is the "coming soon" dead end `16-DESIGN.md` goal 6 forbids. Checked, not

Walk: `?dev=1` (Asheville is unseeded, so the invitation renders) → City tab → Explore.
Scope every query to the Explore tab panel; it is CSS-hidden, not unmounted, when

- [ ] **Step 2: The assertions**

| # | Assertion | Catches |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The invitation section is present on an unseeded place | it never renders at all |
| 2 | It names the place — its text contains the resolved place name, and no `undefined`, `null`, or a bare tab label like "City" | the `placeName` hole |
| 3 | **Zero** `[disabled]` and zero `[aria-disabled="true"]` inside it | prohibited shape 1 |
| 4 | **Zero** elements inside it with a `cursor-not-allowed` computed cursor | prohibited shape 1 |
| 5 | **Zero** `input` and `textarea` inside it | prohibited shape 3 — nothing to select, nothing to type |
| 6 | Exactly one `button` or `a` inside it, and `elementFromPoint` at its centre resolves to it or a descendant | a control that renders and **cannot be clicked** — PR #92 shipped exactly that |
| 7 | Clicking it changes the view observably (a topic detail opens) | prohibited shape 2 — the no-op |
| 8 | Its text matches **none** of `/coming soon|stay tuned|under construction|notify me|join the waitlist/i` | prohibited shape 4 |
| 9 | On Bloomington (`?dev=1&place=bloomington`, City tab) the invitation is **absent whenever the agenda section rendered** | it leaks onto a place that has content |
| 10 | On **Federal** and **Unified** the invitation is absent | nothing to seed; one nation-wide key |
| 11 | Zero page errors and zero console errors | |

Print one line per assertion, exit non-zero on any failure.

Design assertions 3–8 to accept **any element inside any room**, not only the invitation, so
16-09 can point the same script at the reserved rooms by adding selectors and nothing else.

🔴 **Assertion 9 rots, and it must rot into a SKIP rather than a failure.** Bloomington is
"seeded" only while its most recent agenda is inside 16-06's 45-day freshness window. The
council meets every few weeks, so most of the time it is — but between sessions, or if the
pipeline pauses, the agenda module falls back to its reserved room, `isUnseeded` flips to
`true`, and the invitation **correctly** appears. A flat "the invitation is absent on
Bloomington" would then fail while the app is behaving exactly as designed, and a check that
cries wolf on a calendar gets switched off.

So make it conditional and make the condition visible:

```
if (agendaSectionRendered)  assert invitation absent      → PASS/FAIL
else                        print SKIP: "Bloomington has no agenda inside the freshness
                            window today; the invitation is expected and this assertion
                            cannot run."
```

A SKIP is not a pass — print it in a way nobody will read as one, and make the run's exit
code ignore it. The same rot affects 16-10's screenshot matrix: a Bloomington City shot
taken between sessions shows the unseeded page, which is correct and should be labelled, not
retaken until it looks right.

- [ ] **Step 3: Prove it can fail**

Temporarily change the control to `<PrimaryButton disabled>Choose what matters here</PrimaryButton>`
and re-run. Assertions 3, 4 and 7 must go red. Revert, confirm green.

Then try the subtler one: make the button's `onClick` a no-op while leaving it enabled.
**Assertion 7 must still catch it.** If it does not, assertion 7 is checking the click rather
than the consequence, and it is the single assertion this plan exists for — fix it before
moving on.

## Out of Scope

- 🔴 **The seed vote itself.** Recording which issues a member picks is a write, it needs a
  table and a route that do not exist, and *a vote that evaporates on reload is worse than no
  vote*. It is its own plan, sequenced after a real write path exists. Nothing in this module
  stores anything — not in `localStorage`, not in `sessionStorage`, not anywhere.
- **"Request a topic of your own."** Decision 11's second half, and also a write. The
  "arrives next" sentence names it; nothing here collects it.
- **The quiet seeded-case variant.** Considered and deferred: with no vote behind it there is
  nothing for it to offer, and a card offering nothing is the dead end. It ships with the
  vote.
- **A notify-me, a waitlist, or any "tell me when this is ready".** A write, and the
  prohibited shape in its most tempting form.
- **Any moderation, review or promotion workflow** for member-contributed topics. A CONTEXT
  non-goal.
- **Seeding Los Angeles or anywhere else by hand.** Content work the design's non-goals
  defer. Bloomington (16-06) is the one seeded place, and it exists to prove the bespoke
  layer, not to be a content programme.
- Adding a frontend test framework.
